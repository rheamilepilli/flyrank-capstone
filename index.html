Predictive Content Refresh Scoring Engine for Search Visibility

Author: ML Engineering Internship Capstone | Track: Machine Learning (Week 8)

Abstract: Content performance naturally decays over time due to shifting query intent and competitive entry. This research develops an automated refresh opportunity scoring engine to identify high-potential content items undergoing decay before major traffic loss occurs. Utilizing DuckDB over 78+ million anonymized daily performance rows, we engineered historical trajectory and volatility features. A Random Forest regressor was trained on a temporal split to predict click deltas relative to historical baselines. The model achieved a significant reduction in Mean Absolute Error over a zero-change baseline, providing an evidence-backed priority queue for content updates.

1. Introduction & Problem Statement

Content marketing teams waste substantial operational resources manually auditing static landing pages. This project creates an evidence-based decision-support model that ranks pages requiring content refreshes based on predicted organic traffic trajectories rather than arbitrary age metrics.

2. Data Summary

The study utilizes the FlyRank/internship-warehouse release on Hugging Face. The sample subset of fact_content_daily_performance was aggregated at the content item level. Private queries, domain URLs, credentials, and client identifiers were excluded to ensure public safety compliance.

3. Methodology

  • Label Definition: Click delta calculated as avg_clicks_recent - avg_clicks_base over a split target window.
  • Validation Strategy: Chronological/split division to evaluate performance against standard non-change metrics without data leakage.
  • Features: Impression trends, historical baseline click volume, and recent positional variance.

5. Limitations & Honest Framing

This model provides directional decision support for content refresh prioritization. It does not prove causal impact from Google algorithm updates or guarantee specific position recoveries upon updating content.

6. Ranked Recommendations Playbook

  1. Priority 1 (Action Required): High baseline clicks combined with negative click deltas — trigger immediate metadata and intent-match updates.
  2. Priority 2 (Monitor): High positional volatility with stable impressions — schedule for technical internal linking reviews.
  3. Priority 3 (Prune/Merge): Sustained near-zero clicks over 90+ days — flag for consolidation.
